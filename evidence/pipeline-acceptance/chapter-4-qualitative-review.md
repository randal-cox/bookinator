# Chapter 4 qualitative review

Date: 2026-10-16

Fixture: public-domain *The Dunwich Horror*

Model: `qwen2.5:7b`
Disposition: operational and identity-safety checkpoint passed; editorial acceptance rejected

## What improved

The fourth authored specimen completed every production stage and locked the
next bounded checkpoint. Its final combined read retained 14/14 exact anchors,
covered the chapter's late movement, resolved every surviving projection
subject, made no unsupported compact kinship claim, and supplied exact evidence
for all ten applicable Review dimensions.

The consistency boundary now rejects a proposed exclusion unless it addresses
the exact candidate, identity, name, and source evidence. A critic cannot erase
a participant using a vague label. A self-merge is recorded as a no-op rather
than a successful correction. The deterministic closure merged the mutually
aliased `Wilbur` and `Wilbur Whateley` records and rewrote their references,
leaving six unique cast identities and no dangling subjects.

The retry also reused both the immutable primary response and the frozen
consistency audit because their input signatures were unchanged. The recorded
0.1-second closure therefore did not conceal a second expensive model run.

## Why the chapter still fails

The corrected Summary is only a 30-word literal rendering of the final source
movement. It has one key-point record. The corrected Dossier likewise has one
event claim. Both are source-faithful, but neither is a useful account of a
1,241-word chapter.

This exposed an important defect in the earlier assay: exact citations,
referential integrity, and consistency closure can all pass after aggressive
removal has destroyed editorial breadth. Safety from hallucination is not the
same property as usefulness.

The original model output also left semantically false questions behind after
its prose claims were removed, including a question about preparations for
Wilbur's father's death when the dying character was his grandfather. Those
questions are anchored to real passages but remain misleading. The current
collector does not yet score question truthfulness independently; this remains
an explicit open defect.

## Resulting contract change

For a source of at least 500 words, pipeline acceptance now requires a digest
of at least 60 words, three Summary claim records, and three Dossier claim
records. Short sources still require at least one claim in each projection.

The frozen v9 report is therefore red at 25/34. Chapter 4 passes the mechanical,
identity, evidence, ending, Review, and consistency checks but fails
`chapter.5.content-breadth`. Eight older inherited failures remain visible in
Chapters 1–3. This is a real improvement in discrimination, not a green release.

## Next semantic boundary

Add claim-level validation for generated questions and ending-state prose, then
require a bounded repair to restore representative coverage rather than merely
falling back to the last exact source passage. Until that exists, Chapter 4 is
safe enough to inspect but not accepted as useful analysis.
