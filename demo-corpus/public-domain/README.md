# Public-domain pipeline shelf

Eight Project Gutenberg EPUBs chosen to make Bookinator confront different
genres, structures, lengths, voices, and editorial problems. These are source
manuscripts for local evaluation; generated analysis can later become the
public gallery at `book.inator.com`.

| File | Work | Why it is here | Project Gutenberg |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pride-and-prejudice.epub` | *Pride and Prejudice* — Jane Austen | Long social novel; dialogue, irony, many recurring characters | [eBook 1342](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1342) |
| `frankenstein.epub` | *Frankenstein* — Mary Shelley | Nested and epistolary narration; framing, chronology, shifting speakers | [eBook 84](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/84) |
| `the-yellow-wallpaper.epub` | *The Yellow Wallpaper* — Charlotte Perkins Gilman | Short unreliable first-person narration and psychological deterioration | [eBook 1952](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1952) |
| `frederick-douglass-narrative.epub` | *Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass* — Frederick Douglass | Memoir/nonfiction, historical context, causality, and sensitive subject matter | [eBook 23](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/23) |
| `alice-in-wonderland.epub` | *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* — Lewis Carroll | Children's literature, nonsense, wordplay, abrupt scene logic | [eBook 11](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11) |
| `the-time-machine.epub` | *The Time Machine* — H. G. Wells | Time travel, embedded explanation, distant chronology, speculative world rules | [eBook 35](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/35) |
| `the-turn-of-the-screw.epub` | *The Turn of the Screw* — Henry James | Deliberate ambiguity, long syntax, framing, uncertain supernatural claims | [eBook 209](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/209) |
| `a-study-in-scarlet.epub` | *A Study in Scarlet* — Arthur Conan Doyle | Mystery, clues and payoffs, plus a conspicuous mid-book setting/time shift | [eBook 244](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/244) |

Project Gutenberg identifies each selected edition as public domain in the
United States. Its distribution terms remain inside each EPUB. Confirm local
copyright status before publishing outside the United States.

## Suggested queue order

1. *The Yellow Wallpaper* — fast end-to-end smoke test.
2. *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* — short, structurally clear chapters.
3. *The Time Machine* — direct test of chronology and speculative continuity.
4. *A Study in Scarlet* — mystery obligations and structural discontinuity.
5. *The Turn of the Screw* — ambiguity and syntactic complexity.
6. *Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass* — nonfiction and historical voice.
7. *Frankenstein* — nested narrators and letters.
8. *Pride and Prejudice* — long multi-character endurance test.
