Messaging brief

Elevator pitches

Current explanations of the working product, followed by language that keeps its claims honest.

One sentence

Bookinator is a local editorial workbench that reads a manuscript chapter by chapter, connects evidence across the book, and turns the analysis into reports an editor can inspect.

20 seconds

Bookinator gives a long manuscript a durable memory. It builds chapter summaries and dossiers, maps emotions and editorial signals, connects entities and locations across the book, and follows narrative questions toward their payoffs. The manuscript and local model work stay on the user’s computer.

One minute

Bookinator imports a manuscript, proposes its chapter structure, and saves several independent kinds of analysis instead of producing one enormous AI answer. Chapter summaries and dossiers feed whole-book rollups. Emotions, tags, and Smells reveal patterns and sentences worth attention. Connections and Questions & Payoffs combine evidence across chapters. Editors can inspect the run state and source evidence, then export a branded PDF or an offline interactive report. The current release is useful, but it does not prove plot holes, compare revisions, or replace editorial judgment.

Audience-specific openings

Novelist

Bookinator gives you a book-scale view of recurring characters, locations, questions, emotional movement, and passages that may deserve another look.

Developmental editor

Bookinator builds a navigable memory of the manuscript so you can orient faster, inspect distant connections, and spend more time on editorial judgment.

Copy or continuity editor

Bookinator complements search with cross-chapter grouping, presence and absence maps, timeline observations, tags, and source-linked Smells. A deeper contradiction engine remains on the roadmap.

Writing group

Bookinator can create a shared, offline report with summaries, questions, connections, emotions, and retained findings while leaving the manuscript itself out.

Privacy-conscious author

Your manuscript, derived analysis, and model prompts stay on your computer during normal use. Bookinator requires no cloud account or manuscript upload.

Technical collaborator

Bookinator is a public, source-available experiment in local book-scale analysis with saved artifacts, inspectable model runs, failure diagnostics, and reproducible exports.

Language to avoid

  • Do not say Bookinator understands a book, proves a plot hole, or comprehensively checks continuity.
  • Do not claim revision comparison, reviewer annotations, exact claim-level anchoring, or cross-platform installers as current features.
  • Do not describe a model result as an editorial verdict, quality score, or replacement for human reading.
  • Do not promise that every artifact links to an exact sentence. Source precision currently varies by analysis.
  • Do not say “free forever.” State the BSL 1.1 use grant and 2030 GPL conversion precisely when licensing matters.
