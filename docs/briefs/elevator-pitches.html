Messaging brief

Elevator pitches

Short, honest explanations for people who have not seen Bookinator.

One sentence

Bookinator is a free, local tool that remembers the facts and promises in a manuscript, finds places they may break, and links every finding back to the text.

20 seconds

Books create hundreds of little obligations: what happened, who knows what, what was promised, and what still needs to pay off. Bookinator tracks those obligations across the whole manuscript and gives authors and editors evidence-linked questions to investigate. It runs locally and is free forever.

One minute

A manuscript can be perfectly coherent chapter by chapter and still contain a contradiction hundreds of pages apart. Bookinator imports the book, builds persistent records of characters, facts, events, promises, mysteries, and timelines, then looks for continuity gaps, abandoned threads, redundancy, and cruft. It does not hand down an AI verdict or rewrite the author. Every finding is a question with source passages, model provenance, and uncertainty attached. The manuscript stays on the user’s computer, and the project will remain free forever.

Audience-specific openings

Novelist

Bookinator helps you see what a major revision accidentally broke: continuity, character knowledge, setup, and payoffs across the entire book.

Developmental editor

Bookinator gives you a source-linked map of threads and tensions so you can spend more time making judgments and less time reconstructing the manuscript.

Copy or continuity editor

Search finds repeated words. Bookinator is designed to connect meaning across chapters: changed facts, impossible timing, and knowledge a character never acquired.

Writing group

Bookinator turns vague reactions into passages everyone can inspect together, without pretending the machine gets the final say.

Privacy-conscious author

Your unpublished manuscript and model output stay on your computer. Bookinator needs no cloud account and does not upload the book.

Technical collaborator

Bookinator is a GitHub-distributed, local-first experiment in book-length memory: inspectable code, durable narrative obligations, reproducible model runs, and original-passage verification.

Language to avoid

  • Do not say Bookinator understands a book or proves a plot hole exists.
  • Do not promise comprehensive detection or imply that silence means the manuscript is sound.
  • Do not describe a finding as an editorial verdict, quality score, or substitute for human reading.
  • Do not call it private if a future optional integration sends manuscript text elsewhere without explicit, informed consent.
  • Do not turn “free forever” into a funnel for a gated professional edition.
