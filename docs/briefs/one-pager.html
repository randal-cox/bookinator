Product overview

Finding the plot holes before your readers fall in.

A free, local manuscript intelligence tool that remembers a book’s facts, promises, mysteries, and timelines—and links every useful finding back to the text.

The problem

A book can be locally convincing and globally wrong. A detail on page 30 can contradict page 450; a cut scene can orphan a payoff; a character can know something they never learned. Search finds words, but not always these relationships.

What Bookinator does

Import a manuscript, inspect its chapter structure, and run local analysis. Bookinator builds durable dossiers of entities, facts, events, promises, questions, and timelines. It proposes continuity, thread, redundancy, and cruft findings with source passages attached.

Why it is different

Bookinator treats a manuscript as a system of obligations, not a pile of summaries. Findings retain evidence, model and prompt identity, generation history, and uncertainty so authors and editors can inspect the reasoning.

For authors

Check the second-order effects of revision, trace promises and character knowledge, and find contradictions before readers do—without giving up authorial judgment.

For editors

Orient faster, compare distant passages, and turn a hunch into a cited question. Bookinator supports developmental, continuity, and copy work; it does not automate the editor’s verdict.

Private by design

Manuscripts, extracted text, and model runs remain on the user’s computer. Local models run through Ollama. There is no required account or cloud upload.

Free forever, inspectable in public

No subscription, premium tier, or manuscript-data business model. Releases will live on GitHub so people can inspect the code and contribute. Bookinator 0.1.0 is source-available under the Business Source License 1.1 and converts to GPL-3.0-or-later on September 24, 2030.

Boundary: Bookinator can be wrong. A finding is an evidence-linked editorial question, not proof of an error, a quality score, or a replacement for an author or editor.
