BookinatorProject resources

Working roadmap

Things we should want

Product, evidence, community, and communication assets worth pursuing—without turning a fun free project into a company.

Now
  1. 1
    Five excellent test manuscripts
    Different genres, structures, lengths, and revision states, with known problems and explicit permission.
  2. 2
    An editor grading rubric
    Useful, plausible-but-wrong, distracting, and harmful findings; plus evidence quality and time-to-dismiss.
  3. 3
    Golden continuity suite
    Seeded cross-chapter contradictions, missing payoffs, knowledge errors, and timeline failures for repeatable evaluation.
  4. 4
    Five-minute real-product demo
    Import, inspect, run, open a finding, verify its passages, and dismiss or retain it.
  5. 5
    Plain privacy promise
    Exactly what remains local, what logs exist, where projects live, and how deletion and export work.
  6. 6
    These public resources
    One-pager, 20-minute deck, elevator pitches, and an honest roadmap hosted on an inator property.
Next
  1. 7
    Revision comparison
    Show which obligations changed, disappeared, or became unresolved between manuscript versions.
  2. 8
    Editorial finding workflow
    Statuses, notes, false-positive reasons, evidence pinning, and exportable questions for the author.
  3. 9
    Model and hardware guide
    Useful local models, memory requirements, expected speed, and honest quality tradeoffs.
  4. 10
    Portable book project
    One inspectable bundle containing manuscript derivatives, dossiers, findings, run history, and settings.
  5. 11
    Friendly cross-platform launchers
    Double-clickable startup for macOS, Windows, and Linux, with guided Ollama setup, model checks, storage estimates, and clean uninstall paths.
  6. 12
    Editor advisory circle
    A small group who can challenge categories, language, false confidence, and workflow fit.
Later
  1. 13
    Genre-aware obligations
    Mystery clue knowledge, romance arc expectations, speculative-world rules, and nonfiction claims—without forcing formula.
  2. 14
    Open evaluation corpus
    Public-domain and consented manuscripts with documented traps and human editorial judgments.
  3. 15
    Plugin and export boundary
    Scrivener, Word, Markdown, and editorial-report interchange that preserves source locations.
  4. 16
    GitHub contribution workflow
    Inspectable code, contribution guidance, issue templates, tests, and boundaries for shared inator infrastructure.
  5. 17
    Case notes, not testimonials
    Specific examples of what was caught, missed, or dismissed, published only with author permission.
  6. 18
    Sustainable stewardship
    Documentation, contributors, issue triage, and optional donations that never gate capability.

Distribution decisions to make deliberately

Bookinator 0.1.0 is distributed under the Business Source License 1.1, with free personal, educational, research, and internal business production use. It converts to GPL-3.0-or-later on September 24, 2030.

Things we should not want

  • A generic manuscript score, bestseller prediction, or “make it more marketable” dial.
  • Cloud dependence disguised as convenience.
  • A volume metric that rewards more flags over better findings.
  • A premium tier that contradicts free forever.
  • Author replacement, style homogenization, or untraceable rewriting.
  • A launch larger than our evidence.
