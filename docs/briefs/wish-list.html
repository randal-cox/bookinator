Working direction

Next bets and evaluation

What the first milestone proved, what the product needs next, and which larger ambitions remain experiments rather than promises.

Proved in 0.1.0
  1. 1
    The complete local loop
    Import a real manuscript, map chapters, save analysis, reconcile whole-book artifacts, explore the result, and export it.
  2. 2
    Several useful lenses
    Summaries, dossiers, emotions, tags, Smells, connections, and narrative questions can coexist without collapsing into one opaque answer.
  3. 3
    Inspectable long-running work
    Queue state, model identity, timing, attempts, failures, and retries remain visible while local analysis runs.
  4. 4
    Professional outputs
    The saved work can become a branded PDF or a model-free interactive report that excludes the manuscript.
Next
  1. 5
    Reviewer annotations
    Let a human select source text, write durable comments, judge machine findings, and carry those decisions into reports.
  2. 6
    Stable evidence anchors
    Give dossier claims, questions, events, relationships, and editorial decisions durable source ranges that can survive a revised manuscript.
  3. 7
    Grouping review
    Make entity and location merges explicit, reversible, and teachable so pluralization, aliases, misspellings, and type errors do not silently distort Connections.
  4. 8
    Editorial calibration
    Measure useful findings, plausible mistakes, distracting output, evidence quality, and time to dismiss across public-domain and consented books.
  5. 9
    Faster, safer local inference
    Reduce expensive repeated work, add checkpoints, preserve partial progress, and improve bounded recovery from malformed model output.
  6. 10
    Sharper chronology
    Separate narrated order from story time and represent dates, elapsed time, uncertainty, flashbacks, and competing placements.
Aspirations
  1. 11
    Revision comparison
    Show which facts, obligations, questions, and source anchors changed between manuscript versions.
  2. 12
    Continuity engine
    Reconcile character state, world rules, deadlines, objects, relationships, and reader knowledge to propose distant contradictions.
  3. 13
    Multiple plot maps
    Chart action, agency, relationships, mystery pressure, revelations, losses, and setup activity without forcing one universal story curve.
  4. 14
    Author intent contracts
    Let an author state constraints such as keeping a revelation hidden until a chapter, then evaluate revisions against that intent.
  5. 15
    Friendly cross-platform distribution
    Provide guided installation, model checks, storage estimates, updates, and clean removal on macOS, Windows, and Linux.

These are directions, not release promises

The in-app Roadmap is the broader living backlog. This brief calls out the work most likely to change Bookinator’s trustworthiness or editorial usefulness next.

Things Bookinator should refuse

  • A generic manuscript score or bestseller prediction.
  • Cloud dependence disguised as convenience.
  • A volume metric that rewards more flags instead of better findings.
  • Author replacement, style homogenization, or untraceable rewriting.
  • A polished claim that outruns the evidence the product can show.
